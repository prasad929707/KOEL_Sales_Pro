# KOEL Commercial Terms Policy (Annexure 2 & 3, April 2026)

## Offer Validity
- Standard: 30 days from quotation date
- Up to 60 days: permitted with Regional Sales Manager (RSM) approval
- Beyond 60 days: requires Head Office (HO) approval — KOEL prices at delivery apply

## Scope Options
### Supply Only
- Advance: 35% standard (minimum 25%, maximum 50%)
- Balance: 65% via proforma invoice, prior to dispatch
- Below 25%: requires HO approval. Below 35%: document reason + RSM note.

### Supply + Installation & Commissioning (I&C)
- Advance: 50% standard (minimum 40%, maximum 60%)
- Balance: 50% after successful installation & commissioning at site

## Delivery Lead Times
- Small gensets (<82 kVA): 6–10 weeks
- Mid-range (82–320 kVA): 8–12 weeks
- HHP (320–750 kVA): 14–18 weeks (confirm slot with production)
- Optiprime (1010+ kVA): 16–20 weeks (factory-tested unit)
- Minimum delivery: 6 weeks for standard, 12 weeks for HHP, 16 weeks for Optiprime

## Fixed Terms (always apply, not negotiable)
- GST: 18% extra on ex-works price
- Freight & Insurance: customer's liability
- Price Excludes: unloading, shifting, erection, earthing, power cables, first-fill diesel, load bank testing
- Civil Works: DG foundation, cable trench — customer scope
- Cancellation: 2% charges on order value if PO cancelled
- Storage: ₹10,000/month + 15% p.a. inventory burden if delivery not collected after inspection clearance
- Arbitration: Disputes at Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu
- Force Majeure: KOEL not liable for damages from force majeure

## Warranty (Standard)
Period: 2 years or 5,000 hours from commissioning, or 30 months from supply — whichever is earlier
5C engine components: 5 years.
3 mandatory service checks through authorised KIRLOSKAR Care Centre.
Warranty void if: not installed by KOEL-authorized dealer.

## Warranty (Optiprime)
Period: 5 years from date of commissioning.
3 mandatory service checks.
CSPs (Customer Service Packages) available for extended scheduled servicing.

## Scope Boundaries — What KOEL Does NOT Include (without formal amendment)
- Cable laying / power cable supply
- Civil works (foundation, trenches, earthing pits)
- First-fill diesel
- Load bank testing
- Unloading and shifting at site

## Custom / Non-Standard Terms
- Any scope change not in standard Terms of Supply requires RSM approval minimum
- Changes valued above ₹2L: GM-Sales approval required
- Extended warranty beyond policy: explicit HO approval required — rep cannot commit
- KOEL does NOT offer unconditional buy-back guarantees
- Client-scope takeover: must be formally scoped, priced, and approved in writing

## AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract)
- 2 preventive service visits/year + emergency callout
- Response SLA: 4 hours in metros, 8 hours in Tier-2 cities
- OEM-backed (not third-party dealers)
- KRM remote monitoring included on new gensets
