## Sizing Standard
KOEL sizes gensets so block load = 50% of rated kW. This provides:
- Motor starting surge headroom (DOL motors draw 3–6× running current at startup)
- Future load addition headroom
- Prevention of wet stacking (carbon buildup at very low loads)
- Optimal CPCB IV+ engine efficiency band (50–75% load)

Formula: Required kVA = Block Load (kW) × (1/Power Factor) × (1/0.50)
- Office loads (PF 0.92): multiply block load by 2.17
- Mixed loads (PF 0.80): multiply block load by 2.50
- Pumps & motors (PF 0.72): multiply block load by 2.78
- Data centre / UPS (PF 0.90): multiply block load by 2.22

## Deration (IS 10001)
Standard rating at 40°C ambient, sea level (0m MSL).
- Temperature deration: 2% per °C above 40°C
- Altitude deration: 1% per 100m above 1000m MSL
- Both apply simultaneously (multiplicative, not additive)
Example: Nagpur (47°C, 312m) → 14% deration → 500 kVA delivers 430 kVA effective

## AMF (Automatic Mains Failure) Panel
- Mains failure detected in <1 second (all 3 phases + neutral monitored)
- Engine start-to-load transfer: <10 seconds total
- Configurable 3–5s delay to avoid nuisance starts on momentary dips
- Back-feed protection and neutral sensing standard

## KRM — KOEL Remote Monitoring
- Tracks: fuel level, battery voltage, run hours, fault codes, live load graph
- Alerts: SMS + email to facility team and KOEL service
- SIM card included on new gensets — no extra hardware cost
- Helps prevent low-fuel/battery drain emergencies

## Fuel Consumption (BSFC — KOEL CPCB IV+ engines)
- At 75% load: ~0.262 L/kWh (optimal efficiency band)
- At 50% load: ~0.295 L/kWh (part load — slight reduction in efficiency)
- At 100% load: ~0.305 L/kWh (peak, slightly rich mixture)
- At 25% load: ~0.370 L/kWh (very inefficient — wet stacking risk)
Rule of thumb: KOEL ≈ 10–15% below Cummins at equivalent kVA, lower 5-year TCO on sub-250 kVA (no AdBlue).

## CPCB IV+ Compliance
- Full KOEL range is CPCB IV+ certified (India's current strictest emission standard)
- Sub-250 kVA: EGR (Exhaust Gas Recirculation) — NO AdBlue/urea required
- 250+ kVA: SCR (Selective Catalytic Reduction) with DPF
- No urea = lower running cost + simpler operations for facility teams
