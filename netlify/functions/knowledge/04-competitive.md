# KOEL Competitive Positioning

## vs Cummins India
- Price: KOEL typically 10–15% lower at equivalent kVA
- Sub-250 kVA: KOEL significantly better TCO — no AdBlue/urea (Cummins uses SCR across more of their range, requiring urea top-ups)
- Service network: Both have pan-India coverage. KOEL response SLA: 4h metro, 8h Tier-2.
- Product quality: Both premium Indian manufacturers. KOEL has stronger govt/PSU history; Cummins stronger in large industrial and power gen.
- For large projects (1 MW+): Cummins and KOEL are direct competitors. KOEL Optiprime is the counter to Cummins PowerCommand multi-set systems.

## vs Greaves / Powerica
- Primarily rebadge/assembly. KOEL designs and manufactures its own engines — key differentiator for total cost of ownership and parts availability.

## vs Chinese / Korean imports
- CPCB IV+ compliance: Chinese sets often sold as compliant but fail field audits. KOEL is OEM-certified.
- Service: Chinese OEMs have very limited India service network. Downtime cost during service wait is the key argument.
- Total cost: Indian gensets 20–30% higher upfront but 40–50% lower lifetime cost (parts, service, fuel).

## Key KOEL Differentiators
1. Only Indian company supplying emergency nuclear-grade gensets to NPCIL (seismically qualified)
2. 45,000+ telecom tower fleet — proven reliability at scale
3. Indian Navy ₹270 crore Make-I defence contract (2025) for 6MW marine diesel engine
4. CPCB IV+ EGR technology on sub-250 kVA — no AdBlue running cost
5. KRM remote monitoring — no extra hardware, SIM included
6. Optiprime twin-pack — up to 40% fuel saving vs equivalent single-engine set
7. NABH documentation package for healthcare installations
8. 75+ years manufacturing history, Pune-headquartered, pan-India service

## When Competitor Comparison Comes Up
- If asked for exact price comparison: "Get from ERP quotation system — I can confirm KOEL is competitive on TCO."
- If customer has Cummins incumbent: "KOEL AMC can cover the existing Cummins set during transition. We're used to this."
- If customer insists on imported brand: "CPCB IV+ certification is non-negotiable for Pollution Control Board compliance. Imported sets without Indian certification create legal risk."
